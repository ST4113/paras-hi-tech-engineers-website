# Paras Hi Tech Engineers — Business Website

A client website developed for **Paras Hi Tech Engineers**, an Ahmedabad-based supplier of spare parts and components for heavy earthmoving, mining, construction, material-handling and industrial equipment.

🌐 **Live website:** https://parashitech.in/

## About the Client

Paras Hi Tech Engineers serves businesses that operate and maintain heavy machinery. Its product offering includes components and spares intended to support equipment reliability and reduce operational downtime.

The business's areas of focus include:

- Radiators and cooling systems for heavy machinery
- Hydraulic oil coolers
- Engine spare parts
- Air-conditioning spares and components
- Truck body spare parts
- Parts for earthmoving, mining, construction and material-handling equipment

The company supports equipment used across demanding industrial applications and caters to multiple major machinery brands.

## Website Overview

The website provides the business with a digital presence where prospective customers can understand its product categories, explore its offerings and contact the company for enquiries.

### Website Objectives

- Present the company and its industrial spare-parts business.
- Organise product and service information for prospective buyers.
- Make it easier for customers to enquire about machinery components.
- Establish a professional online presence for the business.

## Development Scope

- Business website design and frontend development
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile
- Product and company information presentation
- Search-engine optimisation fundamentals
- Image optimisation and website performance improvements
- Website deployment and domain configuration
